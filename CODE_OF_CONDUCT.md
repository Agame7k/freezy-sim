# Code of Conduct

Freezy Sim is built by and for the FRC community, where students, mentors and volunteers work side by side. We want
everyone who uses or contributes to this project to feel welcome, whatever their age, experience or background.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new: many contributors are students.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully.

It applies in the issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space, and when you represent the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer privately, through the email address on the
[@Agame7k GitHub profile](https://github.com/Agame7k). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.

Many contributors are minors. Behavior towards them that would not be acceptable at an FRC event is not acceptable here.
